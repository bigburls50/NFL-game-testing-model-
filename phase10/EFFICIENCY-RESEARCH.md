# Phase 10 efficiency challenger (research only)

Production stays on `phase10-causal-logistic-v2`. Neither challenger passed the predeclared promotion gate. This is an actual negative result, not an unavailable or fabricated forecast.

- Team challenger: V2 plus recent offensive and defensive passing/rushing EPA per recorded play.
- QB challenger: team challenger plus prior completed-game starter's weighted quarterback EPA and completion percentage above expectation. The upcoming starter's retrospective identity is never used to construct historical features. This does **not** validate a live starter-change adjustment.
- Teams and quarterbacks join on exact completed game IDs and confirmed stat identities; franchise aliases are resolved across relocations. Opposing team stats supply defensive EPA allowed. Same-date update batches exclude contemporaneous results.
- Fit each evaluation season on earlier seasons only, warming up in 2016. Use 2023–24 for development and compare 2025–26 holdout with V2. The holdout overlaps V2's previous retrospective evaluation and is not an independent prospective test.
- The locked week 11 slate compares V2 and the two challengers on identical eligible games. Two games have a difference between previous-game starter and current projected starter; the comparison records the distinction rather than pretending the projected player was historically known in advance.
- No wager, stake, return or recommendation is generated by this challenger. The existing BetMGM paper test is unchanged.

## Reproduce

Download the nflverse schedules CSV and the weekly team/player CSVs from the URLs and checksums in `efficiency-validation.json`. Store stats as `team-2016.csv`, `player-2016.csv`, through 2026, in one directory.

```sh
node phase10/evaluate-efficiency.mjs /path/to/games.csv /path/to/stats
node --test phase10/*.test.mjs
```

Do not overwrite `efficiency-shadow-week11-2026.json`. It was locked before kickoff; the separate settlement report is refreshed using only final-score records.
